In the shop

“My name is Aaron Juetten and I started my own taxidermy business - Put’em Up Taxidermy in 2019.

I live in Foley, Minnesota with my wife and three kids. Hunting and fishing are a priority in our home. My wife gave me the nudge I needed to try taxidermy and now I do what I love every day. I completed a taxidermy course and was also mentored by a great life long taxidermist. If you can put’em down, we can put’em up! Send a message or call if you would like a memory recreated.”
